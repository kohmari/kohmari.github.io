Hi! I am a **postdoctoral researcher** at the [Sustainable Economy Research Group, at CentraleSupélec, Université Paris Saclay](https://www.serg.paris/). I work at the intersection of economics, management science and environmental engineering, and investigate the **design, fairness and effectiveness of corporate climate initiatives**. I completed my [https://theses.fr/s367297](PhD at CentraleSupélec) in **April 2026**. 

Funded by Stellantis, my current research centers on game theory and optimisation to model **the diffusion of Net Zero commitments across multi-tier, imperfect upstream supply chains**. I also punctually investigate other related topics, such as **the voluntary carbon market, carbon markets, sustainable innovation, and ecolabel design**.
